# Generated ranking and family artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool (not the CLI). Each asset was generated separately with a transparent background, visually inspected, and resized to a 384px PNG for the game. Original full-resolution generations remain separate from this deployable project. Alpha transparency is preserved.

## Final project assets

- `public/assets/icons/rank-gold.png`
- `public/assets/icons/rank-silver.png`
- `public/assets/icons/rank-bronze.png`
- `public/assets/icons/family-bee.png`
- `public/assets/icons/family-oak.png`
- `public/assets/icons/family-barn.png`

The same trophy images are used by all player leaderboards and Family Tournament standings. The three new family emblems are available when creating a family and in leader-only Family management. Six further emblem options reuse existing painted illustrations: Pumpkin, Green beans, Cheese, Apple juice, Berry preserves and Harvest hamper.
