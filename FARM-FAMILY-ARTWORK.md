# Farm Family artwork

Four separate icons were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and integrated as transparent 384 × 384 PNG game assets. They match the existing warm painted farming illustrations; no icon was substituted with a line drawing.

| File in `public/assets/icons/` | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `family-weekly-order.png` | This week tab and Family Order header |
| `family-members.png` | Members tab, welcome page, members header and topbar button |
| `family-tournament.png` | Tournament tab and prize-pool header |
| `family-management.png` | Family tab and management header |

Style prompt common to all four: a single centered, fully visible transparent-background game icon for Harvest Tycoon, warm polished hand-painted 3D cartoon farming inventory artwork, rounded friendly forms, saturated earthy colours, golden wood grain, soft upper-left lighting, carefully painted highlights, readable at 48px, slight three-quarter view, generous margins, no scenery, enclosing frame, text, numbers, logos or watermark.

Subject prompts:

1. Family weekly order: a wooden harvest delivery crate with a round loaf, amber honey jar with cream cloth lid and twine, bright corn and golden wheat, and a rolled parchment tied to its front with a green ribbon.
2. Members: two friendly adult farmers, a woman with a brown ponytail and a man with dark skin, wearing straw hats and forest-green overalls, shown waist-up holding a small basket of fresh apples together.
3. Tournament: a golden harvest cup with two handles, corn relief, dark wooden pedestal, wheat ears, green leaves and a forest-green award ribbon.
4. Management: a small golden wooden noticeboard with an arched roof, cream parchment bearing three green checkmarks, a brass farmhouse key on a hook and a leafy sprig tied with twine.

The four reattached reference PNGs were visually confirmed to be these same illustrations. Full-resolution generated originals were retained in the conversation. Runtime copies preserve transparency and are reduced to 384px for faster mobile loading.

## Family Hall icon (v2 correction)

`public/assets/icons/familyhall.png` is a new, separate transparent PNG. It replaces the Farmhouse alias in the icon registry and the building catalogue. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then exported at 384 × 384 pixels for the game.

Prompt: one small inviting rural community hall with a distinctive rich blue pitched roof, cream timber walls, a broad wooden front porch and steps, porch posts, warm windows, and a green pennant with a golden wheat emblem. Entire building shown in an elevated isometric three-quarter view. Polished hand-painted stylized 3D farming illustration, golden timber, blue roof, green accent, soft upper-left light. Compact centered composition, generous padding, legible at 40px. Transparent alpha background. No surrounding land, scenery, ground tile, UI frame, people, lettering, numbers or watermark.
