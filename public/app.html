About the game

Harvest Tycoon is a free 3D farming game. Plant and harvest crops, bake and craft goods in your own buildings, sell them at the Market and grow a small farm into a big estate.

  • Play right in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no download, or get the free app for Android on Google Play.
  • An app for iPhone is coming soon.
  • The free app for iPhone is on the App Store.
  • Play together in a Farm Family of up to 10 farmers: fill the Family Chest every week, take part in the weekly family tournament and chat with farmers from all over the world.
  • The game is in 16 languages.
  • Free to play: you can buy diamonds if you like, for VIP and boosts, and you also earn diamonds by playing.
  • Made by Millstone in the Netherlands.
