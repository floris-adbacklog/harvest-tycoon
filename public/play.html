WELCOME, FARMER!

A little guide to growing

  1. Plant something good

    Choose a crop below, then tap an empty field. On desktop, tablet or phone, hold the mouse button or your finger down and drag to move the farm in any direction. Click or tap to work; pinch with two fingers to zoom. Seeds cost coins.

  2. Give it a little care

    Water once to reduce remaining growing time by 20% and add one crop to the harvest. Use the Care tool after the first 30% of the original growing time for one more crop and another 15% off the remaining time. A fully cared-for harvest also earns double XP.

  3. Bring in the harvest

    See a golden basket? Tap the crop or its basket to harvest, then sell your produce at the market. Dragging moves the view without harvesting.

  4. Make more from your harvest

    Tap a building or open Buildings. The Feed Mill makes animal feed and sunflower oil. The Windmill grinds wheat and barley into grain meal, then refines it into flour for fresh bread and fresh pumpkin pie. It also makes natural fertilizer and larger batches of flour and feed. Use fertilizer from the Windmill menu to reduce a crop’s remaining time by 35%, once per planting. Feed the cows and chickens, then use their milk and eggs in the Bakery. Collect each batch when it is ready.

  5. Grow your little empire

    Upgrade buildings for faster production. Production buildings now reach level 10. Visit the Farmhouse to unlock fields one at a time, up to 24. You start with 4 wheat and 2 feed so you can try production straight away.

  6. Make yourself at home

    Tap the tractor, or open More → Tractor, to work all your fields. Research at the silo for cheaper seeds and faster growth. Visit the cart for delivery orders, and click your level badge to open your journal.

  7. A little reason to return

    Open Today for your daily gift and three new challenges. Collect coins and diamonds on consecutive days for a seven-day reward cycle. Open Boosts to spend diamonds on farm bonuses. Diamond purchases become available after beta. Missing a day restarts the streak; your farm and earned rewards stay yours.

  8. Build for the long run

    Open Estate for projects lasting from two hours to seven days, followed by repeatable commissions. Earn mastery medals for 25, 100, 300 and 1,000 harvests of each crop. Your farm stall earns a smaller income while you are away; chores, crop care and production reward active play.

Your progress saves automatically in this browser. Crops and production keep growing while you are away. Ready crops never wither. The farm stall stores 24 to 48 hours of earnings, depending on its level. Daily gifts, challenges and orders refresh at 00:00 UTC. The leaderboard shares only your name, coins and level. Clearing browser storage removes this device’s farm.
