How it works

  1. Make your partner account. With your email address and a password. You get your own link right away.
  2. Share your link on your website, your channel, in your videos or on social media. Use the assets below.
  3. A new player starts with your link. Their device remembers it for 30 days, until they make their farm. From then on they are yours, for good.
  4. They buy diamonds or a pack. 25% of the price without VAT is yours. Example: a pack of €4.99 is €4.12 without 21% VAT, so you earn €1.03.
  5. From €20, ask for a payout in your panel above. We pay it by hand and mark it paid.

Partner terms

  • You earn 25% of the price of every purchase, without 21% VAT, made by players whose farm started with your link. Purchases by your own account and test purchases do not count.
  • A player counts for you when their new farm starts within 30 days of opening your link on that device. A player who already had a farm, or who started with someone else's link first, does not count.
  • Payouts are made by hand, on request, from €20, by bank transfer or PayPal. Give the name and account the money should go to. We can ask for more details before we pay.
  • A purchase that is refunded or charged back does not count. We can hold back or reverse a payout for fraud, for example fake accounts, buying through your own link or misleading players.
  • No spam, no misleading claims, no ads that bid on the name "Harvest Tycoon", and nothing aimed at children under 16.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes on what you earn.
  • We can change or end the programme. What you earned before that stays yours and can still be paid out.
