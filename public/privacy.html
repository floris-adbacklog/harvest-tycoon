2. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password. Your password is stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider; we cannot read it.
  • Your player name (chosen by you, or picked for you at random) and the avatar you choose.
  • When your account was created, whether you confirmed your email address, and when you last signed in.
  • Technical sign-in data kept by our sign-in provider for security, such as your IP address, browser type and session times.

Your game

  • Your farm and progress: fields, crops, buildings, inventory, coins, diamonds, experience points, level, quests, achievements and statistics.
  • A record of recent game actions with their times, so that the server can check them and repair problems.
  • When you were last active, used to show whether you are online.
  • Where and on what you last opened the game: your IP address, your browser and device type, the country of your device’s time zone and the language you play in. We use this to keep the game fair and secure (for example to spot one person playing with several accounts) and to see where our players come from and which languages they play in. Only your latest visit is kept: each new visit replaces it.
  • Farm families: the families you join or create, your role, your contributions, invitations, and the help, gifts and requests you exchange with family members.
  • Events: whether you took part, your progress, your ranking and your rewards.
  • The chat: the messages you send (in the global chat, your family chat or a private chat), with your player name, avatar and the time; which chats you have read; the farmers you block; reports you make about a message; and your chat settings. If a moderator mutes or bans you from the chat, we keep that decision and its reason.
  • Feedback, bug reports and feature requests you send with the Feedback button: your message, whether it is feedback, a bug or a feature request, and with it your player name, level, device (your browser's user agent), game language and the time. Our team and the moderators read them.
  • Gifts or corrections we add to your account ourselves, for example after a support request.

Purchases

When you buy diamonds, a pack, a special offer or a season pass such as the Halloween Pass on our website, you pay on a checkout page run by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details and any information it needs for the payment (such as your email address) under its own privacy policy. We receive and keep: what you bought, the price and currency, the payment status, the dates, and Stripe’s reference numbers for the checkout and the payment, linked to your account. We do not receive your full card number.

In our Android app (version 1.1 and later) you pay through Google Play instead. Google collects your payment details under its own privacy policy and is the seller of record for that purchase. We receive and keep: what you bought, the payment status, the dates, and Google’s references for the purchase (the order number and the purchase token), linked to your account. We check every purchase with Google before your farm gets it. When Google refunds a purchase (for example when you ask Google for a refund), Google tells us, and we take back what the purchase gave as far as your farm still has it. See The Android app.

Reminders (only with your permission)

  • Push notifications: if you allow them, we store the push address your browser gives us (a web address and encryption keys), your browser or device type, and whether delivery worked. In the Android app push notifications go through OneSignal instead, and we only keep whether you turned them on; see The Android app.
  • Daily email summary: if you switch it on, we send it to your account email address.
  • News and offers by email: only if you switch on News & offers by email in Settings, or tick the box for it when you confirm your email address. Both start off. We keep whether you said yes and when you last changed it.
  • Your reminder choices, your time zone and your preferred hour, and when we last sent you a reminder.

Push notifications only arrive after you allow them on your device. Once allowed, the reminders for private messages and mentions, your daily gift and crops & goods ready are on; email reminders and news and offers by email are off until you switch them on. The daily gift reminder also looks at when you last played: after 3 and 6 days away it tells you a comeback chest is waiting. You can change each of them at any time in the game under Settings.

When you contact us

If you write to us with our support form or by email, we keep your message, your email address and our reply for as long as we need them to help you.

Website visits

When you visit the website, our hosting and database providers process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages or services requested, in order to deliver them and keep them secure. Google Analytics, the Meta Pixel and the TikTok Pixel also receive data when you visit; see section 8.

How you found us

When your farm is created, we record once how you found Harvest Tycoon: the campaign tag in the link you followed (such as src, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign and utm_content), the name of the website that linked to us (only the domain, such as reddit.com, never the full address), whether the link carried a click ID from Facebook or Instagram, TikTok or Google, as their ads and some of their posts add (only yes or no, never the click ID itself), whether you came over from the Facebook, Instagram, Threads or TikTok app, the page you arrived on, your game language and your device type. Nothing for this is stored on your device: the page keeps it in memory until your account is made, and if you sign in with Google or Facebook or confirm your email, it goes along in the address of the page you come back to. If you sign up with an email address, it is also saved with your account right away, so it still counts if you confirm your email on another device. Only our team sees it, as totals per link, to learn which places bring farmers who enjoy the game.

Playing on CrazyGames

You can also play Harvest Tycoon on CrazyGames (crazygames.com). There you never sign up with us: no email address and no password.

  • Logged in with CrazyGames: CrazyGames gives the game a signed token with your CrazyGames user ID and username (and a link to your CrazyGames profile picture, which we do not use). Our server checks the token and keeps the user ID with your farm, so you find the same farm wherever you log in to CrazyGames. Your username, made to fit our name rules, becomes your farmer name (a name you chose yourself as a guest stays); you can change it like any farmer name. We do not receive your CrazyGames password or email address.
  • As a guest: if you are not logged in, we make a guest account for your farm, without a name, email address or any other details from you. The way back to it is kept in your browser (see section 8), so clearing your browser’s storage loses it. When you log in with CrazyGames later, the guest farm becomes the farm of your CrazyGames account.
  • Everything else is as for any farm: your game data (above), that your farm came from CrazyGames (How you found us), and what other players can see (section 5). The chat is only open to players logged in with CrazyGames, and only when CrazyGames allows chat. To limit how many new guest farms one network can start, our server keeps a scrambled form (a keyed hash) of your IP address for at most two hours, never the address itself.
  • No trackers from us: on CrazyGames we load no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel or other trackers, show no ads of our own, set no cookies and send no emails or push notifications. CrazyGames runs its own website and software under its own privacy policy and is responsible for what it collects.
  • Deleting your farm: email info@harvesttycoon.com with your farmer name (and your CrazyGames username if you logged in), and we delete your farm and the data linked to it.

This is not available in the Android app or the iPhone app; there you sign in with your email address and password.

  • If you choose Google or Facebook, that service asks you to agree to share your name, email address and profile picture with Harvest Tycoon, together with a unique identifier for your account at that service.
  • We use the email address and the identifier to create your account and to sign you in. Our sign-in provider stores the name and profile picture with your sign-in details; the game does not show them. Other players see only the player name and avatar you choose in the game.

Google and Meta process your data under their own privacy policies. You can remove Harvest Tycoon’s access at any time in your Google account or Facebook settings. That stops future sign-ins through that service but does not delete your Harvest Tycoon account; see section 11 for that.

5. What other players can see

Harvest Tycoon is a multiplayer game. Other signed-in players can see your player name, avatar, level, your game statistics on the leaderboards, whether you have VIP, and whether you are online. Members of your farm family also see your contributions and the help, gifts and requests you exchange. Event rankings show the player name, avatar and level of the top players. Your email address is never shown to other players.

The chat. Messages in the global chat can be read by every signed-in player, family messages by the members of that family, and private messages only by the two farmers in that chat. Moderators (players we trust to keep the chat friendly) and we can read a message that someone reports, and can delete it or mute or ban its sender from the chat; a chat ban never affects your farm. When a farmer mentions you in the global or family chat (@ and your farmer name), the message keeps your player id and farmer name with it, so the name opens your profile, and it reaches you like a private message. You can block a farmer, and you can switch private messages off under Settings. Please do not share personal details such as your address or phone number in the chat.

Choose a player name that does not reveal more about you than you want to share. You can change it at any time under Settings.

Invite a friend. Your invite link contains a short code, not your email or account ID. If someone starts Harvest Tycoon with your link, you see their player name and level in your list of invited friends, and they see your player name. When they reach level 10 within 30 days, you both receive diamonds.

Share my farm. The picture is made on your device from your farm view. It shows your player name, level, family name and flag, and your invite link. It is not sent to us; you choose where to share it.

The privacy policy and account deletion pages do not load these tools.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: for as long as your account exists. We may delete accounts that have not been used for a long time.
  • When you delete your account in the game: we delete your personal data or make it anonymous at once, except the data below that we must or may keep. If you ask us to delete it instead, we do so within one month.
  • Purchase records: for as long as Dutch tax and accounting rules require, generally seven years.
  • Chat messages: 30 days, then they are deleted automatically. Notifications in the chat: at most 60 days. Reports that have been handled: 90 days.
  • Feedback, bug reports and feature requests: one year, then they are deleted automatically.
  • Push notification addresses: until you switch push notifications off or sign out on that device, or until the push service tells us the address no longer works.
  • Notifications in the Android app: whether you turned them on, for as long as your account exists. OneSignal keeps a phone's push token and device data for as long as we use OneSignal, unless we delete them earlier. Signing out in the app ends the link between that phone and your player ID; when you delete your account, we also delete the data at OneSignal that is linked to your player ID.
  • Daily email summary: until you switch it off or unsubscribe.
  • Emails with us: for as long as needed to handle your question, and afterwards only if we need them for legal reasons.
  • How you found us: for as long as your account exists.
  • Playing on CrazyGames: the link between your CrazyGames user ID and your farm, for as long as your account exists; the scrambled IP address of a new guest farm, at most two hours.
  • Technical logs: for a limited period, according to the settings of our hosting and database providers.
  • Analytics data: according to the retention settings of Google Analytics, Meta and TikTok.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced.

We may keep data for longer where that is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to prevent fraud or abuse.

10. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • ask which personal data we hold about you and receive a copy (access);
  • have incorrect data corrected (rectification);
  • have your data deleted (erasure);
  • have the use of your data restricted (restriction);
  • object to our use of your data based on legitimate interests (objection);
  • receive the data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format (data portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example for reminders or cookies, without affecting earlier use.

To use these rights, write to us with our support form or email info@harvesttycoon.com from the email address linked to your account, or include enough information for us to find your account. We may ask for more information to confirm that the request comes from you. We answer within one month; if a request is complex or we receive many, this can be extended by two more months, and we will tell you if that happens. Requests are free of charge, but if a request is clearly unfounded or excessive, we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse it, as the GDPR allows.

Some things you can do yourself at any time in the game under Settings: change your player name, switch reminders on or off, sign out, and delete your account.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and the personal data linked to it yourself at any time, in the game under Settings, Privacy, Delete account, on the website, in the Android app and in the iPhone app. After a warning you type your farmer name to confirm, and your account is deleted at once. If you can no longer sign in, ask us through our support form from the email address linked to your account. What is deleted and what is kept is explained on our account deletion page.
