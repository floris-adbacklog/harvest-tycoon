Delete your account

Last updated: 4 October 2026

You can delete your Harvest Tycoon account and the personal data linked to it yourself, at any time, in the game. This works the same way whether you signed up with an email address, with Google or with Facebook, on our website, in the Harvest Tycoon app for Android on Google Play and in the Harvest Tycoon app for iPhone on the App Store.

Delete your account in the game

  1. Sign in and open your farm on harvesttycoon.com, in the Android app or in the iPhone app.
  2. Open Settings, go to Privacy and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and tap Continue.
  4. Type your farmer name exactly as it is and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted at once: you are signed out and you see “Your account has been deleted.” on the home page. There is no waiting period and it cannot be undone.

Can’t sign in any more?

Send us a message with our support form from the email address linked to your account (if you signed in with Google or Facebook, that is the email address of your Google or Facebook account), with your farmer name, and ask us to delete your account. We may ask you to confirm the request, so that nobody else can delete your account. We delete it and confirm by email within one month, usually much sooner.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in details: email address, password, and any link to your Google or Facebook account.
  • Your player name, avatar, farm and all your progress, inventory, coins, diamonds, VIP, medals, statistics and leaderboard entries.
  • Your place in your farm family, your contributions, rewards, event entries, and help, gifts and requests. If you lead a family, the longest-standing co-leader (or else the longest-standing member) becomes the leader; a family you were the last member of ends.
  • Your chat messages, your private messages (from both sides of the conversation), reports you made and farmers you blocked.
  • Your invites: your invite code and the links with friends you invited or who invited you.
  • If you are a partner: your partner account, and partner earnings that have not been paid out yet. Request a payout first and wait until it has been paid; payouts already made stay in our records.
  • Your notifications, reminder settings, push notification addresses and daily email subscription, and for the Android app the data at OneSignal (our push notification service) that is linked to your player ID. The iPhone app uses OneSignal in the same way.
  • Where and how you play (device, country, language), how you found us, and the history of your farm.

Where a record also belongs to other players, for example a family request you helped with or a chat report about a message, we keep it without anything that identifies you. Your farmer name can still appear in the text of other players’ own messages, notifications and farm history until those are deleted automatically, after at most 90 days.

What we keep

  • Purchase records (what was bought, the amount, the date and the payment references of Stripe or Google Play) for as long as Dutch tax and accounting rules require, generally seven years. In our records they are no longer linked to your account, your email address or your player name; Stripe and Google keep their own payment records under their own privacy policies. Purchases are not refunded when you delete your account.
  • Data we need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Deleted data can remain in backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced.

Deleting your account is permanent. Your farm, progress, coins, diamonds and purchased items cannot be restored afterwards.

Signed in with Google or Facebook?

Removing Harvest Tycoon from the apps connected to your Google account or Facebook account stops future sign-ins through that service, but it does not delete your Harvest Tycoon account or game data. To delete those, use Delete account in the game as described above. Afterwards you can also remove the connection:

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → Harvest Tycoon → Remove.
  • Google: your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Harvest Tycoon → Delete all connections.

Menu names can differ slightly depending on your app version.
