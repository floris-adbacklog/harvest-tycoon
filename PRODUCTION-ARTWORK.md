## Instant harvest

File: `public/assets/icons/instant-harvest.png`

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. A single polished inventory icon for the cozy farming game Harvest Tycoon, representing Instant harvest: three small parallel raised rows of rich brown soil on one rounded compact plot, with ripe golden wheat in the back row, bright corn with green leaves in the middle, and two small orange pumpkins in front. A bold magical gold lightning bolt floats above the field with two tiny gold sparkles, suggesting all crops ripen instantly. Warm hand-painted 3D casual farm-game style, chunky rounded forms, rich material shading, golden sunlight, crisp edges, simple silhouette legible at 48 pixels. Centered three-quarter isometric view with transparent padding. Square image, genuinely transparent alpha background. No crate, no seed bag, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no frame, no scenery, no UI card, no watermark. Only this isolated field-and-lightning icon.

The crate is used for per-building collection. The field is used only for the whole-farm crop boost in the diamond shop. Finish one crop retains its existing seed-bag illustration. No boost costs or paid-purchase behavior changed.
